# Dead Air on the Meridian

*Case 1 of "I Hear Everything", a voice-interrogation murder mystery.*

December 1936. The Meridian Limited sleeper train is stopped for the night by a snow slide below
Kestrel Pass. Desmond Crane, the most feared gossip voice in American radio, has been found beaten to
death in his drawing room. His sign-off is *"This is Desmond Crane, and I hear everything."* A rotary
plow will reach the train at dawn with the county sheriff aboard. After that the passengers scatter.

You are the railroad's special agent. You have until six o'clock, a gravel-voiced conductor and a
dining-car table. Everyone on Crane's car had a reason to fear him and everyone is hiding something,
but only one of them killed him. You can't talk anyone into confessing. You read how they sound, you
find what's real, and you prove it.

## How to play

```bash
python3 scripts/casepack/play.py examples/case-packs/dead-air-on-the-meridian
```

Run it from the repository root. It works fully offline, with no API keys: type plain questions
("where were you when the train stopped?", "what time is it, Colonel?", "who typed this letter?"),
examine the scene, present evidence against a statement, link cards on the board, and when you're
sure, go to Briggs's Gate and accuse. The clock is always running. Asking, pressing, searching and
presenting all cost minutes. Eight endings, one of them right.

Typical play time is 20–40 minutes.

**Spoiler warning:** `NARRATIVE.md`, `VO-DIRECTION.md`, `GDD.md`, `TECH.md`, `ART-BIBLE.md` and
`case-file.json` contain the solution. Don't open them before you play.
